How it works

  • Add a video: record one in the app, choose one from your library, or send one to SloMo from another app.
  • Hold the video to slow it down. Let go and it carries on at full speed.
  • While holding, drag right to speed up and left to slow down. Keep going left to freeze, then further to rewind.
  • Choose the hold speed (1/2, 1/4 or 1/8) in settings, where you can also turn on voice commands and vibration.

Common questions

Why does my clip look jerky when slowed?
Slow motion needs extra frames. Clips filmed at 60, 120 or 240 frames a second stay smooth. SloMo tells you how slow each clip can go when you add it.

Where are my videos kept?
On your phone, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I remove a clip?
Use the remove button on the clip in your library. This deletes the app's copy, not the original in your phone's gallery.
