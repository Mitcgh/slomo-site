SloMo app privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

SloMo is an app from SloMo Technology Ltd, a company based in the United Kingdom. This page explains what the SloMo app does with your information. The short version: your videos stay on your phone, and we do not collect anything about you.

What stays on your phone

Videos you record in SloMo or add to it are copied into the app's own storage on your phone. So are their thumbnails, your settings, and the extra copy the app makes of a clip so that rewind runs smoothly. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Removing a clip in SloMo deletes the app's copies. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

What we collect

Nothing. SloMo has no account, no sign-in, no adverts and no analytics. We do not receive your videos, your voice, your location, your contacts or any identifier for you or your phone.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to record video in the app.
  • Microphone: to record sound with your videos, and to hear voice commands if you turn them on.
  • Photos and videos: so you can choose a video to slow down.
  • Speech recognition: to turn spoken commands such as pause and play into actions.

You can refuse any of these and still use the rest of the app. You can change them at any time in your phone's settings.

Voice commands

Voice commands are off until you turn them on. When they are on, the microphone listens only while you are holding a video and for a few seconds after you let go. The words are recognised by the speech service built into your phone, provided by Apple or Google. Depending on your phone's settings, that service may process the audio on its own servers under its own privacy policy. SloMo never receives or stores the audio.

Children

SloMo is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of SloMo starts to collect any information, this page will be updated before that version is released.
